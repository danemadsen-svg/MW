**Short version: Wardrobe doesn't collect any data about you.** Everything you put in the app stays on your iPhone.

## What stays on your device

Item photos, your catalog, outfits, your avatar and its reference photos, and try-on renders are all stored only in Wardrobe's storage on your iPhone. They are processed only on your iPhone: auto-tagging uses Apple's built-in Vision framework, and image generation uses a model running on your device.

Photos are re-encoded when you add them, which removes location and camera metadata.

We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive your photos or any information about you.

## The image model download

If you choose to download the optional image model, your iPhone connects once to huggingface.co (Hugging Face, Inc.) to fetch it. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to Hugging Face, which handles it under its own privacy policy. Nothing about you or your closet is sent. After the download, image generation works offline.

## Purchases

The Wardrobe Lifetime purchase is processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. Apple confirms the purchase to the app on your device.

## Backups

Your Wardrobe data is part of your device backup (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on, and is handled by Apple under its own terms. The downloaded image model is excluded from backups.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Erase All Data removes everything Wardrobe stored. Deleting the app also removes it.

## Children

Wardrobe isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anyone's data.
